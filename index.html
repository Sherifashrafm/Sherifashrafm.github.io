One data platform for after-sales, inventory and sales

ERP data in one governed analytics platform, serving dashboards in production.

Problem

Inventory, after-sales, warranty and sales data sat in separate places. Stakeholders had no single, trusted view.

My role

I built the core of the platform and designed its architecture. Colleagues contributed to parts of it.

Business insight

Each department argues about different numbers, so I shaped the data around the KPIs people use in meetings, not around SAP table names.

Solution

A governed, layered analytics platform that keeps every report in step with its source. It feeds dashboards for inventory, after-sales, sales funnel, warranty and customer retention.

Result

Dashboards in production across the business, and more reliable, more accurate data for decision-making.

Tools

SAP S/4HANA, SQL, Power BI, workflow automation.

AI demand planning for spare parts

Far fewer false urgent purchase orders across four brands.

Problem

Urgent purchase orders were raised for parts that did not need them, adding cost and noise to procurement.

My role

I designed the forecasting approach and built the core engine, with support from colleagues.

Business insight

An urgent order usually points to a planning gap, not a real shortage. So the goal was earlier visibility of demand, not faster rush buying.

Solution

Machine-learning forecasting with anomaly detection and clustering, covering thousands of spare parts for Jeep, Subaru, Alfa Romeo and Geely.

Result

False urgent orders fell sharply, and procurement decisions became more accurate.

Tools

Python, machine-learning forecasting, SQL, ERP data.

Predictive service reminders

A model that forecasts each vehicle's next service visit.

Problem

Customers were reminded on fixed rules, so many were contacted at the wrong time and many were never contacted.

My role

I built the model and the SAP posting integration, and specified the logic for the SAP developers.

Business insight

Retention depends on reaching a customer when the vehicle is due, which differs by vehicle, brand and driving pattern.

Solution

A machine-learning model trained on years of historical service visits that predicts when each vehicle will return.

Result

Validated against history, with the SAP posting integration built and trialed. It supports proactive outreach and customer retention.

Tools

Python, machine learning, SQL, ERP integration.

SAP S/4HANA migration and operational control

Business lead for the migration, plus the SOPs and audits that kept after-sales consistent.

Problem

After-sales had to move from SAP ECC to S/4HANA without disrupting service, parts or finance, while processes differed between branches.

My role

I was the key business user and lead for after-sales, linking operations, finance, IT and BASIS.

Business insight

Because I had worked jobs in SAP as an advisor and managed them as a manager, I tested the system the way people really use it.

Solution

I migrated vehicle, parts, customer and service-history data, ran user acceptance testing, and built ABAP/ALV KPI reports. I also standardized SOPs, introduced an incentive scheme and ran monthly audits.

Result

A migration with no disruption to service, parts and finance, and a clear rise in customer throughput from a retention study and win-back campaigns.

Tools

SAP S/4HANA and ECC, ABAP/ALV, Power BI, SQL, SAP Signavio, MS Visio.
